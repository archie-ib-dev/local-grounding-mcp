# Contributing

Issues, bug reports, documentation improvements, tests, and code contributions are welcome.

## License for contributions

Unless a separate written agreement applies, by submitting a contribution for inclusion in this repository, the contributor agrees that the accepted contribution may be distributed under `AGPL-3.0-only`.

The contributor must have the legal right to submit the contribution. Do not submit employer-owned, confidential, copied, or otherwise restricted material without authorization.

## Alternative licensing

The project may offer alternative licensing in the future. A Contributor License Agreement may therefore be required before accepting contributions intended for inclusion in an alternatively licensed edition. The requirement, if applicable, will be disclosed before such a contribution is merged.

Submitting a pull request does not transfer copyright ownership by itself.

## Security reports

Do not disclose suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue. Follow `SECURITY.md`.
